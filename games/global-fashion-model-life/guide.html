👗 Global Fashion Model Life — Walkthrough

Objective & Career Progression

Start in New York or Metro Manila, customize your Three.js 3D model, book jobs, build followers and reputation, and unlock all 8 fashion capitals (from New York/Manila up to Milan at 105 rep and Paris at 150 rep).

  • 5 Ranks: New Face (0 rep) → Working Model (14) → Fashion Week Regular (45) → International Talent (105) → International Headliner (230).

Controls

  • Mouse & Touch: Click/tap tabs (Studio, Bookings, Challenges, Camera, Passport, Lifestyle, Career), adjust face/body/makeup controls, and drag horizontally on the 3D portrait to rotate your model.
  • Keyboard: Press Space (or tap the runway button) to hit timing cues during Runway bookings.

Styling, Challenges & Saves

  • Wardrobe & Daily Trends: Browse 55 garments across 6 styles (Minimal, Casual, Tailored, Evening, Craft, Editorial). Matching the daily trend adds a +20% booking fee bonus!
  • Camera & Saves: Save up to 12 Looks and export a 12-photo Contact Sheet as JPEG. Career saves in localStorage (global-fashion-model-life-v1).
