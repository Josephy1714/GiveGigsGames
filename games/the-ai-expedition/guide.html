🧭 The AI Expedition — Walkthrough

Objective & Node States

Link every orbiting AI Agent, Data Lake, and Automation node to the core before the stage countdown hits zero (28s starting clock, +11 to +17s per cleared stage, capped at 45s).

  • Stage 2+ Cycling States: Blue (Ready) = +120×combo pts; Gold (Surge) = +250×combo pts & +2s; Red (Firewall) = -3s & resets combo (max 5×).

Controls & Saves

  • Mouse & Touch: Click or tap an outer node to connect it to the network.
  • Keyboard: →/↓/Tab next node; ←/↑ previous node; Space/Enter connect selected node; P/Esc pause; F fullscreen.
  • Saves: High score saves to localStorage (the-ai-expedition:best).
