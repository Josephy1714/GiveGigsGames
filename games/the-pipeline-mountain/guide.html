⛰️ The Pipeline Mountain — Walkthrough

Objective & Biomes

Climb as high as possible across 4 altitude zones—Basecamp (0m+), Cloud Deck (50m+), Stratosphere (120m+), and Orbit Peak (200m+)—without falling below the camera.

  • Platforms: Blue System (standard bounce), Green Spring (1.75× mega bounce), Cyan Moving (20m+), and Red Fragile (crumbles after 1 bounce).
  • Pickups & Hazards: Grab Gold Medals (+1, or +2 at 3+ streak) and Orange Jetpacks (2.2s upward thrust above 18m). Above 40m, stomp Purple Bug Drones from above for a 1.45× bounce and +3 medals—hitting them from below ends the run!

Controls & Saves

  • Keyboard & Touch: A/D or ←/→ (or touch controls) steer horizontally with left/right screen wrapping; jumping is automatic upon landing on a platform. P/Esc pause; F fullscreen.
  • Saves: Best score saves in localStorage (the-pipeline-mountain:best).
