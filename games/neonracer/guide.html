🏎️ Neon Racer — Walkthrough

Objective & Scoring

Race down an endless 3-lane cyberpunk highway, dodging incoming traffic for as far as possible. One crash ends the run.

  • Bonuses & Pickups: Thread past cars closely (<22px horizontal gap) for +15m near-miss bonuses. Grab $ coins and 4 pickups: Speed (⚡), Slow (🐢), Shield (🛡️ +100m bonus), and Magnet (🧲 +5 coins).

Controls & Saves

  • Keyboard: ←/→ or A/D switch lanes; ↑/↓ or W/S accelerate/brake (2×–8×); Space start/restart; P/Esc pause; M mute; F fullscreen.
  • Touch: Tap left/right halves or swipe lanes, use on-screen ◀ ▶ ▲ GAS ▼ buttons, or enable TILT gyroscope steering.
  • Saves: Best distance saves in localStorage (neonRacerHighScore).
