👾 Neon Invaders — Walkthrough

Objective & Wave Structure

Clear endless 4×8 formations of descending alien invaders before they reach the bottom line or reduce your 3 lives (max 5) to zero.

  • Invader Rows: Row 0 holds Tank invaders (3 HP, 30 pts), Row 1 holds Zigzag invaders (2 HP, 20 pts), and Rows 2–3 hold Basic invaders (1 HP, 10 pts). Formation speed scales up with each wave.

Controls

  • Keyboard: WASD or Arrow Keys move your ship in the lower half of the arena; Space fires; P/Esc pause; M mute; F fullscreen.
  • Touch: Drag anywhere to steer, hold FIRE, or toggle AUTO: ON/OFF (auto-fires while dragging by default).

Power-Ups & Saves

  • 4 Drops (15% chance): Triple Shot (10s 3-way spread), Shield (10s hit protection), Rapid Fire (8s high fire rate), and Extra Life (+1).
  • Tip & Saves: Trim the outer columns early so the formation takes longer before dropping toward you. High score saves to localStorage (neonInvadersHighScore).
