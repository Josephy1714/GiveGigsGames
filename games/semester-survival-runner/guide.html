🎓 Semester Survival: The University Run — Walkthrough

Objective & Semesters

Run through 8 Kenyan university semesters (1,000m each) to graduate and unlock Semester 9: Endless Alumni Mode. Keep your Energy above zero while dodging 8 obstacle types and fighting 8 Semester Bosses.

  • Boss Schedule: Telegraphed Semester Bosses (First CAT through Final Exams) appear starting at 1,800m and every +1,600m thereafter, each with 3 telegraphed attacks.

Controls

  • Keyboard: A/D or ←/→ switch lanes; W/↑/Space jump; S/↓ slide (0.9s); Esc pause.
  • Touch: Swipe left/right to switch lanes, up to jump, down to slide.

Power-Ups, Upgrades & Saves

  • 7 Power-Ups: Coffee, Shield (blocks -32 energy obstacle hits), Magnet, 2× Multiplier, Boost, Notes, and Mega-Coffee.
  • Progression & Saves: Unlock 10 characters, 10 permanent upgrades (Lv 0–10), GPA tracking (0–4.0), and 16 achievements. Saves in localStorage under semester-survival-runner-v1.
