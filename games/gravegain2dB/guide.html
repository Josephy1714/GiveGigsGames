🔫 GraveGain2dB: Breach MoonRock — Walkthrough

Objective & Modes

Scout and fight left-to-right through seeded destructible MoonRock fortresses, rescue stranded allies as extra lives, and reach extraction.

  • Routes & Clocks: Play Procedural Roguelite (12 segments across 8 biomes) or Wave Mode (8 segments, 5 waves) in Real-Time, Chrono-Lock, or Turn-Based.
  • Visual Bands: Selectable Kids, Teens, or Adult visual presentation.

Controls

  • Keyboard & Mouse: A/D or ←/→ move; Space/W/↑ jump, wall-jump, or climb; Mouse or I/J/K/L 360° aim; Left-Click fire; Right-Click/Q grenade; Shift dash; E rescue crew; F melee; C build ability; 1/2 or Wheel swap starter/scavenged weapon; P/Esc pause; R redeploy.
  • Touch & Gamepad: On-screen touch buttons plus native gamepad support (Left Stick move, Right Stick aim, RT fire, LT grenade, A jump, B dash, X rescue, Y melee, LB ability).

Rescues, Demolition & Scoring

  • LIFO Crew Lives: Every ally you rescue (E) joins your life stack with a random Race × Class build. When you fall, you respawn as your most recent rescue before falling back to your starting build (plus 1 Giggles save at 1 HP).
  • Demolition Tech: Blow up fuel trucks (chain blasts) and the unstable bridge at 52% map length to crush enemies below. Keep an eye on limited ammo for your 2nd weapon slot.
