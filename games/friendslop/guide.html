🍔 FriendSlop — Walkthrough

Objective & Modes

Keep the Vibe Meter (starts at 100, passively drains -0.1/tick) from hitting zero! Catch falling slop, throw food up to feed your 5 friends (😂🤪🎉🔥💀), and dodge falling cringe hazards.

  • Modes: Play solo in 1 Player or share one keyboard in Co-op Mode (Player 1 🤖 + Player 2 🦊).

Controls

  • 1P Keyboard: A/D or ←/→ move; Space/W or Mouse Click throw slop.
  • 2P Co-op Keyboard: P1 uses ←/→ + Space; P2 uses A/D + W/Shift.
  • Touch: Virtual SLOP-STICK, 🍝 SLOP throw button, 🚀 YEET 3-way spread (800ms cooldown), or tap directly on a Friend to feed them. P/Esc pauses; F toggles fullscreen.

Scoring, Tips & Saves

  • Vibe & Combos: Catching slop adds +2 Vibe (+10×combo pts); feeding Friends with throws grants +8 Vibe, +3 combo, and +50×combo pts. Missing slop drains -5 Vibe, and hitting cringe hazards (😬🤢💩🚫) drains -15 Vibe and resets combo.
  • Saves: Best score saves in localStorage under friendslop-high-score.
