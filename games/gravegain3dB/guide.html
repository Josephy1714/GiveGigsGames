💀 GraveGain 3D B — Walkthrough

Objective & Arsenal

A fast 3D dark-fantasy looter-shooter featuring 23 FPS weapons, procedural weapon rolls (Maker/Barrel/Feed/Rarity/Behavior), 10 Boss Armory signatures, 8 Skill Trees (72 nodes), 16 Synergies, and a flying Cat or Dog battle drone.

  • Modes: 10-Mission Campaign, Endless Dungeon, Realtime/Chrono-Lock/Turn-Based tempos, LuckyStarShip Hub (incl. Service Pistol Workbench), and optional Spire Siege (?match=).

Controls

  • Movement & Parkour: WASD/Arrows move; Mouse look; Shift sprint forward / dodge sideways or back; C slide while sprinting (jump within 0.32s to slide-hop with Flow Shot buff) or crouch; Ctrl crouch; Space jump / mantle ledge / S+Space drop through deck / wall-run; Z/X lean.
  • Combat & Drone: Left-Click fire; Right-Click ADS; R reload; T M4 auto/semi; Y 40mm launcher; G grenade; V class attack; N/Mid-Click weapon bash; 1–4/Wheel swap; 5–7 pick starter cache; U swap drop; J favorite gun (max 8); E pet drone (heals & 1 revive); F race ability; Q potion; B Wait (Turn-Based); P/Esc pause.
  • Touch: Crypt-stick + drag-look + Spell Wheel + 16 action buttons.
