🪨 The Cave of Bottlenecks — Walkthrough

Objective & Targets

Protect your 3 cave shields across escalating waves by aiming the Green Cat's 3D searchlight to melt bottlenecks before they hit the cavern floor.

  • Hostiles: Red Bottlenecks (+120×combo pts, +16% Overdrive) and Wave 2+ drifting Purple Spikes (+220×combo pts, +24% Overdrive).
  • Friendly Emerald Crystals: Do NOT melt them! Letting an Emerald Crystal reach the floor grants +1 shield (up to 3), +28% Overdrive, and +80 pts.

Controls, Overdrive & Saves

  • Keyboard, Mouse & Touch: A/D or ←/→ (or pointer/touch drag) sweep searchlight; Space unleashes 5s Wide-Beam Overdrive when charged to 100% (2.4× beam width, 2.2× melt speed); P/Esc pause; F fullscreen.
  • Saves: Best score saves in localStorage (the-cave-of-bottlenecks:best).
