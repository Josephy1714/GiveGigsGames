💼 Venture Mechanically — Walkthrough

Overview & The 6 Simulators

An interactive educational suite of six startup finance and governance simulators built for Venture Mechanics.

1. Exit WaterfallSandbox + 4 founder challenges (FanDuel Wipeout, Valuation vs. Clean Terms, Debt Trap, Secondary Rescue) + Handbook.
2. Entity ChoiceCompare Delaware C-Corp double taxation against Free Flow LLC pass-through founder distributions.
3. Down RoundTest Broad-Based Weighted Average vs. Full Ratchet anti-dilution protection during a down round.
4. BoardroomSimulate a 2-2-1 board swing vote using lobbying actions and King vs. Rich governance playbooks.
5. SAFE StackModel 2 stacked SAFEs / Convertible Notes conversion dilution plus the Section 1202 QSBS tax clock.
6. Vesting & 83(b)Explore 4-year vesting, 1-year cliffs, early departure, Restricted Stock vs. Options, and 83(b) elections.

Controls

  • Mouse & Touch: Switch simulators from the top navigation, toggle terms and collapsible groups, adjust sliders or −/+ steppers, tap quick-exit chips ($25M, $100M, $250M, $500M), and swipe horizontally across mobile ledger cards.

Walkthrough & Key Takeaways

  • Waterfall Priority: Senior Debt is always repaid before preferred equity, and participating or 2×+ liquidation preferences eat common founder proceeds first.
  • Try the 4 Challenges: Open Exit Waterfall Challenges first—they give concrete target payouts so you can see how one term change flips a wipeout into a win.
  • Saves: Runs purely in-session (no localStorage saves yet).
