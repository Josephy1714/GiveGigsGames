🏁 Overtake — Walkthrough

Objective & Modes

Chain clean overtakes, slipstream boosts, and tight corner drifts across 6 routes in this Babylon.js + canvas hybrid 3D racer.

  • 4 Modes: Race, Time Trial, Knockout, and Endless, each playable in Forward or Reverse across Rookie, Pro, and Ace.
  • 6 Routes: Desert Run, Urban Sprint, Neon Night, Alpine Summit, Cyber Coast, and Stadium Circuit. All are open from your first race in either direction.

Controls

  • Keyboard Use the arrow keys or WASD to steer, accelerate, and brake. Hold C or Ctrl for the handbrake, Space, Shift, or N for nitro, and E, Q, F, or Enter to use an item. Press R to restart or P or Esc to pause.
  • Touch: On-screen Steer Left/Right, Gas/Brake pedals, Nitro slam, Item trigger, and optional Gyro Tilt toggle.
  • Gamepad: Native controller support — Left Stick steer, RT/A throttle, LT/B brake, X/RB nitro, Y/LB item, Start pause.

Garage, Pickups & Items

  • 6 Cars & Upgrades: Unlock Comet 90 ($0), Veloce S (700), Strada GT (1,700), NX Prototype (3,400), Apex V12 (5,200), and Hyperion X (7,800), plus Engine, Nitro, Tires, and Paint tuning.
  • 16 Drivers Pair Human, Elf, Dwarf, or Orc with Warrior, Tank, Support, or Mage. Each pair modifies acceleration, cornering grip, braking force, and nitro duration. The Drivers menu shows how each compares with the car’s base stats.
  • Powerups & Kart Items: Grab 6 powerups (Nitro, Shield, Airstrike, EMP, Magnet, Overdrive) and 12 active Kart items (Rocket, Missile, Oil Slick, Bubble, All-Star, Ice Beam, EMP Storm, etc.) while dodging oil, water, and sand.

Strategy & Saves

  • Tips: Brake before tight corners, steer into the bend, and countersteer to straighten out. Keep your tires on the pavement for the best grip. Draft behind traffic and save Nitro for straightaways. Complete the 10 Career Achievements for 150–600 coin payouts.
  • Saves: Stored in localStorage under pseudo3d-racer-save.
