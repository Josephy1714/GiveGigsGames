🗝️ GraveGain2DC — Walkthrough

Objective & Modes

Master 2.5D dark fantasy combat across a 10-Mission S–F Graded Campaign, Endless Floors (with 5th-floor Wardens), Speedrun Mode, and Dungeon Duel.

  • 3 Tempos: Realtime, Chrono-Lock, and Turn-Based (each move, shot, melee, guard, or wait advances the enemy turn).

Controls

  • Keyboard & Mouse: WASD move; Mouse aim; Left-Click fire weapon; Hold/release V charge melee (breaks breachable walls & triggers Shield Bash during guard flash); Right-Click timed block (guaranteed crit counter); Space dodge/wait; Arrows directional dodge/TB step; E interact/overload Array Conduits; F ability; Q cycle weapons; G explosive; K shove; 1/2/3 tempo; P/Esc pause.
  • Gamepad: Full native controller support (Left Stick move, Right Stick aim, RT fire, LT explosive, A dodge/wait, B melee, X interact, Y ability, LB block, RB cycle weapon, Start pause).
  • Touch: Floating crypt-stick, tap-to-move toggle, holdable Melee rune, and weapon bag.

Secrets, Arsenal & Prestige

  • 20 Weapons & 14 Relics: Hunt weapon caches and level-up relic drafts (such as Blood Oath, Soul Siphon, and Phoenix Protocol). Aim direct shots through humanoid heads for 2× criticals.
  • Secret Vaults: Watch for glowing violet seams on walls and smash them with charged melee, gunfire, or explosives. Watch for Vault Stalker ambushes!
  • Bosses & Endgame: Overload cyan Array Conduits (E) during Lucifer Hades' phase shifts. Earn Career Milestones (30 total), Career Talents (7), Weapon Mastery, and Ascendant Prestige (P0–P5) at Lv 100.
