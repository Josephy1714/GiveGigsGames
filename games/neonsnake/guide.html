🐍 Neon Snake — Walkthrough

Objective & Rules

Guide your neon snake across a 30×30 grid, eating glowing orbs to grow and score without colliding with walls or your own tail.

  • 4 Food Types: Normal (pink, +10 pts), Bonus (yellow, +25 pts), Speed (+ cyan, +15 pts, speeds up tick rate), and Slow (− green, +10 pts, slows tick rate).
  • Milestones: Every 5th segment of length awards an extra +25 points.

Controls & Saves

  • Keyboard: Arrow Keys or WASD steer (uses a 2-turn buffer to prevent accidental self-collision); Space start/restart; P/Esc pause; M mute; F fullscreen.
  • Touch: Swipe in any direction (>24px) to turn; tap to start or restart.
  • Saves: Best score saves in localStorage (neonSnakeHighScore).
