📱 Platform Wars: Phone vs Desktop — Walkthrough

Objective & Modes

Knock your rival off the arena platforms to build score. First fighter to 7 KOs (or leading when the 90-second clock expires) wins the match.

  • Modes: Quick Match vs AI (Rookie, Pro, Apex), Practice Arena, and 2P Local Versus on one shared keyboard.
  • 3 Arenas: Sky Decks, Neon Pit, and Twin Towers (features a horizontally moving center platform).

Controls

  • 1P Keyboard: A/D or ←/→ move; W/↑/Space double-jump; S/↓ fast-fall aerial stomp or drop through upper platforms; F Dash Strike.
  • 2P Local Versus: Player 1 uses WASD + Space jump + F dash; Player 2 uses Arrow Keys + Shift/Enter dash.
  • Touch: Twin-thumb on-screen pad — left thumb ◀/▶ (double-tap within 300ms to dash), right thumb ▲ JUMP and 🔥 FIRE dash.
  • Gamepad: Works via the arcade gamepad adapter (Left Stick/D-pad move, A jump).

Combat & Pickups

  • Damage % & Knockback: Hits raise opponent Damage % (up to 250%), launching them farther on the next blow. Dash Strikes deal 22 dmg; Aerial Stomps deal 16 dmg in a 115px shockwave.
  • Arena Pickups: ⚡ Hyper (8s speed + faster dash cooldown), 🛡️ Shield (blocks 1 hit), and 💥 Surge (8s 1.55× damage and knockback).

Strategy & Saves

  • Tips: Save your double-jump to recover from edge launches, contest 💥 Surge spawns in the center, and use S stomps when an opponent camps beneath you.
  • Saves: 4 career slots stored in browser localStorage (platform-wars:slot0 through slot3) tracking wins, KOs, and streaks.
