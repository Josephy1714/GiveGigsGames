🌀 GraveGain 5D: Multiverse Transcendence — Walkthrough

Objective & 6 Arroyos

Sink 10 multiverse golf holes across 6 parallel Arroyos (Prime Array, Echo Expanse, Dream Shallows, Void Maw, Bloom Lattice, Static Storm) while slaying lane enemies and managing Paradox.

  • 3 Classes: Void Putter (Chain Leap), Paradox Warden (Anchor Reality: -25 paradox, clears doom, +4 HP, 2t ward), and Universe Drifter (Free Hop).
  • Multiplayer: Supports optional 8-player Hop-Race overlay via ?mmorpg=.

Controls & Saves

  • Keyboard: Space/Enter putt (also strikes nearest foe); F melee; U/V hop universe; R class ability; H heal & -6 paradox; Q/E wait/step; T rewind last putt (+12 paradox); G Giggles Help.
  • Touch: Left joystick (flick = step, hard push = power putt 0.5–3×), stage swipes (horizontal hop, up ability, down potion), radial Summon Wheel, and bottom action bar.
  • Saves: Stored in localStorage under gravegain5d_save_v1.
