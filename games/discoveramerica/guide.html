🗽 Madi AI: Discover America — Walkthrough

Objective

Sail westward with Madi AI across 5 Atlantic ports from the English Channel to the American Coast. At each port, sort 8 scattered data fragments into the 3 matching cargo/navigation bins to chart the next leg of the voyage.

Controls

  • Mouse & Touch: Tap or click the pulsing port on the voyage map to open its sorting puzzle. Tap a fragment then tap a category bin, or drag-and-drop the fragment directly into a bin. Tap a placed fragment inside a bin to return it.
  • Buttons & Fullscreen: Use Reset to clear your current bins, Confirm to submit your sort, and Leave to return to the map. Press F for fullscreen.

The 5 Voyage Ports

1. English ChannelLeave Dover by sorting ship cargo, crew safety items, and navigation supplies.
2. Open AtlanticInterpret ocean swell, wind, and sky bearings on the open sea.
3. Mid-OceanOrganize storm-watch observations, rations, and celestial readings.
4. Caribbean ApproachSort island wildlife, currents, and coastal landfall cues.
5. American CoastComplete final harbor charting and coastal landing records to finish the voyage.

Scoring & Strategy

  • Scoring: Each cleared port awards 200 + 8 × 25 = 400 points (2,000 points total across all 5 ports).
  • Free Retries: There is no game-over loss state. Submitting an incorrect placement shakes the board and marks wrong pieces with Move this so you can swap them without restarting the voyage.
  • Tip: Place the clearest clues first to shrink the remaining choices for trickier fragments.
