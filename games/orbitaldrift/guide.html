🛸 Orbital Drift — Walkthrough

Objective & Loop

Pilot a satellite around a central planet in Three.js 3D space. Hold thrust to climb into higher orbits, release to let gravity pull you inward, collect yellow stardust, and survive 4 escalating waves.

  • Risk Zones: Orbiting low in the Inner Burn (r < 75) multiplies score by 1.5×; skimming the Outer Void (r > 155) multiplies score by 2.0× and graze bonuses by 1.5×.

Controls

  • Press V or C, or tap the camera label, to cycle through Solar View, Third Person, and Forward.
  • Keyboard: Hold Space thrust outward, release to fall inward; 1/2/3 switch ships (when inactive); R quick start; H Hangar; P/Esc pause; M mute; F fullscreen.
  • Mouse & Touch: Hold Left-Click or touch to thrust; drag vertically to guide orbit radius or horizontally to nudge angle; quick tap (<250ms) triggers Dash (6s cooldown, 3× pickup radius). Mobile also includes a Gravity Slider, Dash button, and optional Gyro Tilt.

Ships, Debris & Powerups

  • 3 Ships: CubeSat (75 HP, nimble, 1.25× score), Dart (100 HP, 1.0×), Station (150 HP, large pickup radius, 0.9×).
  • 6 Debris Types: Asteroid, Comet, Weaver, Stalker, Splitter (breaks into 3 shards), and Darter (telegraphs before lunging).
  • 5 Powerups: Magnet (7s), Shield (8s + 25 HP), Overdrive (10s, 2× pts), Cloak (3s phase + 2× graze), and Chrono (5s slow debris). Graze debris (<22 dist) to keep your combo alive up to 8.0×!
