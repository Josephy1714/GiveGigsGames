🧟 Last Words Zombies — Walkthrough

Objective & Modes

Decrypt approaching 3D cyber-zombies by typing their code words before they reach the containment barricade (worldZ ≥ 4.8) and drain your 100-point Shield Integrity.

  • Maps & Difficulty: Choose between Fantasy and Cyberpunk 3D arenas across Easy (0.6× speed), Normal (1.0×), and Hard (1.4×).

Controls

  • Keyboard: Type A–Z to lock onto the matching zombie and complete its word; Backspace drops your current target lock; 1 Shockwave Bomb, 2 Cryo Freeze (5s), 3 Shield Restore (+40 HP); hold Shift or Space for 3× zombie speed (risk/reward); Enter start/next wave; I open Black Market from menu; Esc pause; F fullscreen (outside active play).
  • Touch: Built-in #lwz-mobile-deck QWERTY keyboard with ⌫, BANISH/STUN/HOLY power buttons, speed toggle, system keyboard fallback, and tap-on-zombie lock-on.
  • Gamepad: Native controller support — D-pad cycles letters (Left/Right ±1, Up/Down ±6), A types/starts, B clears lock, LB/RB/Y trigger powers 1/2/3, Menu pauses.

Zombies, Combos & Black Market

  • 5 Archetypes: Walker (100 pts, 20 dmg), Runner (125 pts, 1.5× speed, 15 dmg), Ghost (150 pts, weaves, 2 coins), Brute (250 pts, 30 dmg, high knock-resist), and a Boss every 5th wave (1,000 pts, 10 coins, 35 dmg).
  • Chain Shockwaves & Combos: Finishing a word triggers an 8-unit AoE knockback/stun shockwave, builds your combo up to 5.0×, and grants a random powerup every 12 kills (max 3 each).
  • Wave Cleared Glossary & Store: Review decrypted words between waves and spend earned coins in the Black Market on 6 Plasma colors, 3 Fonts, and 2 Music tracks.

Strategy & Saves

  • Tips: Lock onto short-word Runners first so their AoE kill shockwave pushes back nearby Brutes. Save Shockwave (1) and Cryo (2) for every 5th-wave Boss.
  • Saves: Stored in localStorage under gg_coins, gg_high_score, gg_best_wpm, gg_best_streak, gg_runs, gg_total_kills, and equipped cosmetic keys.
