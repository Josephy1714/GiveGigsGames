🎹 Sound Painter 2 — Walkthrough

Overview & Studio Layout

A 4-track, 16-step Web Audio synthesizer workstation with live master oscilloscope and 256-FFT spectrum analyzer. Switch between 4 tabs: Pattern, Mix, Sound, and Library.

  • 4 Tracks: Lead (C5–G3 pentatonic; selectable saw/square/triangle/sine), Bass (C3–C2 triangle), Chords (8 polyphonic 7th pads), and Drums (8 synthesized voices: Kick, Snare, Closed/Open Hat, Clap, Rimshot, Cowbell, Woodblock).
  • 4 Built-in Presets: Neon Synthwave (110 BPM), Techno Pulse (128 BPM), Ambient Dreams (80 BPM), and Chiptune Groove (135 BPM).

Controls

  • Mouse & Touch: Click/tap grid pads to toggle notes; drag vertically on a pad (>12px) to sculpt per-step velocity (0.15–1.0). On compact screens, use the Steps 1–8 / 9–16 and Upper / Lower toggles.
  • Keyboard: Space Play/Stop sequencer; C clear active track; R reset all tracks & synth settings; Enter/Space toggle focused cell.

Synth FX & URL Sharing

  • Sound Shaping: Adjust BPM (60–200), Lowpass Filter Cutoff & Resonance, Envelope Decay, Delay (Time, Feedback, Mix), Convolver Reverb, and per-track Volume & Pan.
  • Share Songs: Open the Library tab to generate a compact ?data=v2... URL containing your full 4-track arrangement and synth settings.
