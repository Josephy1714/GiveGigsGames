🎓 Semester Survival: Jay's University Adventure — Walkthrough

Objective & 4 Districts

Guide Jay across 8 university semesters in a 2.5D anime life adventure spanning 4 connected districts: Residence, Township, Campus, and City.

  • Endings: Complete Semester 8 to earn Honours & Wellbeing (GPA ≥ 3.5 & Wellbeing ≥ 45), Resilient Graduate (GPA ≥ 2.0), or Hard-Earned Graduate.

Controls

  • Keyboard: A/D or ←/→ move (I/K depth forward/back); Space/W/↑ jump; S/↓ slide; Shift sprint; E/Enter interact; Tab open Student Phone; Esc close menus or pause; F fullscreen.
  • Touch: Swipe left/right to move, up to jump, down to slide; tap hotspots or use the on-screen ◀ ▶ ⚡ ▲ E buttons.

Academics, Student Phone & NPCs

  • Course Grading: Each semester tracks 5 courses weighted as 55% Exam + 20% CAT + 15% Assignment + 10% Study toward your 0.0–4.0 GPA.
  • 10 Student Phone Apps: Press Tab to check Home, Timetable, Courses, Academics, Assignments, Assessments, Finances ($2,500 starting cash), Messages, Map, and Weather.
  • Wellbeing & Relationships: Balance Energy, Hunger, Stress, and Wellbeing (0–100) while building bonds with 8 characters including Brian, Amina, Wanjiku, Mama Njeri, and Dr. Mwangi.
