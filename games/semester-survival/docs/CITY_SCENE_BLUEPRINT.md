# Nairobi City Scene Blueprint

This is the district-specific 2.5D layout contract for the wider Nairobi opportunity district. It is a level-design and environment-art handoff, **not a finished scene**. Coordinates are provisional until aligned to approved human-authored artwork.

## Visual identity

Nairobi City should feel broader, more urban and more opportunity-focused than the township or campus. Build a contemporary commercial and institutional district with layered architecture, public pedestrian spaces, carefully designed storefronts and office frontage. Avoid generic glass-tower shorthand: scale, materials, signage, street furniture and urban details must give the environment a credible local identity.

## Scene footprint

- Design world: 3840 × 1080 units; camera viewport: 960 × 540.
- Ground plane: y=850; depth range: -180 to +180; depth scale: 0.58.
- Player bounds: x=70 to 3770; camera horizontal travel: 0 to 2880.
- Three layers: distant skyline, commercial/public-space midground, foreground pavement and street furniture.
- Lighting states: early morning, midday, late afternoon and night.

## Four destinations

1. **Career Centre:** reception, opportunity display and consultation seating for career advice and internship research.
2. **Interview Suite:** professional waiting area and interview-room doors for authored interview gameplay.
3. **Bookshop Arcade:** bookshop frontage, window display and shelves for student purchases.
4. **Innovation Hub:** collaborative work tables, glass meeting rooms and a project showcase wall for networking and project pitches.

The four destinations form a connected west-to-east navigation spine. No city NPC has been invented: the canonical world currently defines no city-based NPC anchor. Add one only after a character and schedule have been authored and approved.

## Artist and level-design handoff

- Create separate background, midground and foreground artwork with consistent perspective and parallax.
- Give each destination a distinct architectural silhouette and readable signage.
- Design believable public circulation, accessible entrances, waiting areas and safe pedestrian edges.
- Use original fictional signage and opportunity boards. Do not use real company logos or fabricate real vacancies, interviews or messages.
- Align every hotspot and collision boundary to the final approved paintover.
- Deliver source files, exact dimensions, measured byte sizes, creator, licence and asset-level credits.
- Weather overlays must not obscure signage, entrances or navigation.

## Gameplay layout contract

The machine-readable blueprint contains four canonical destinations, four navigation nodes, three connected route edges, four spawn points, two collision boundaries and two movement zones. These are provisional layout data; they do not create city NPCs, vehicle traffic, interview content, job listings or production artwork.

## Readiness

Artwork is explicitly not supplied and runtime readiness is false. No asset paths are fabricated. Integration requires approved human-authored layered artwork, hotspot and collision review, credited local assets, browser preview and device QA.
