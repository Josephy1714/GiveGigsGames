# Residence Scene Blueprint

The residence is the player's first home and the emotional anchor of the opening semester. This is a machine-readable layout contract and human production handoff, not finished scene artwork.

## Visual target

Follow the two user-supplied campus references for environmental finish: carefully composed wide establishing views, convincing architectural depth, clean perspective, detailed but controlled materials, rich natural foliage, believable daylight, soft atmospheric light, crisp edges and intentional colour grading. The first reference's landscaped institutional courtyard and the second reference's modern campus walkway are quality cues, not assets to copy.

Translate that finish into a recognisably Kenyan student residence. Do not import Japanese signage, cherry blossoms, Japanese architecture or generic foreign campus details. Use locally plausible building construction, security grilles, water tanks or service details only where confirmed by the final art direction. The room must feel occupied and personal rather than a generic luxury hotel.

## Layout and route

The scene is a 1920 × 1080 design world with a 960 × 540 viewport. The route links:
1. **Shared Room:** Jay and Brian's two beds, two desks, storage and personal belongings.
2. **Courtyard:** shared outdoor gathering and circulation space.
3. **Laundry Corner:** a sheltered practical service area.

Brian has one authored NPC anchor in the shared-room area. The graph connects the room to the courtyard and laundry route. Three entry spawns support the opening, township return and campus return.

## Artist deliverables

- Three separately authored layers: background, midground and foreground.
- Room furniture and belongings must be individually readable and consistent across camera movement.
- Show a clear, believable route between the room, courtyard and laundry.
- Preserve private-room framing and respectful sightlines.
- Lighting variants: early morning, daylight, late afternoon and night.
- Align every hotspot, collision boundary and gameplay zone against the approved final paintover.
- Provide local source files, measured dimensions/bytes, creator and licence records for every asset.

## Runtime contract

The blueprint declares three canonical world places, Brian's anchor, a four-node navigation graph, three collision boundaries, two movement zones and three spawn points. All geometry is provisional until artist/level-design review. The blueprint does not create or imply playable art.
