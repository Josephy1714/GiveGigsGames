# Ownership and Credits

## Ownership principles
Attribute concept and direction to actual project contributors. A tool or AI system is not a human author or rights holder. Do not claim exclusive ownership of third-party or generated assets without verifying applicable terms.

## Required records
For every code, art, font, sound and writing contribution, record creator, source, date, licence/permission and required attribution. Preserve dependency notices.

## Existing repository material
Before removing or substantially rewriting legacy files, inspect repository history and repository licence. Reuse only material whose terms permit reuse in this fork and any upstream contribution. The existing game metadata says “Private - All Rights Reserved”; reconcile that with repository contribution terms before publishing a licence claim.

## Release gate
Final game metadata and notices must accurately describe ownership and licence. Do not invent a licence, imply GiveGigsGames endorsement or remove required attribution.
