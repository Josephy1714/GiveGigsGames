# Visual Production Bible

## Locked presentation direction
The game retains its 2.5D exploration camera. Its visual identity combines cinematic anime environmental composition with premium anime-game character readability and production discipline. High-resolution source art is authored for crisp presentation; runtime output scales to the device and must not assume every phone can render at native 4K.

## Production principles
- Character and environment artwork are separate, versioned deliverables.
- Main cast: Jay, Amina, Einstein, Ernest and Sumeya. Existing supporting cast remains.
- Amina and Sumeya require distinct, beautiful Muslim-appropriate designs with modest contemporary clothing and individually designed hijab styling.
- Jay's locked first-year baseline remains in CHARACTER_BIBLE.md. Four-year maturity remains a separate deferred milestone.
- Characters require model sheets, expression sheets, animation atlases, clip coverage, in-game scale review and approval before integration.
- Each district requires layout paintover, background/midground/foreground art, prop artwork, lighting variants, hotspot review and credits.
- No generated placeholder art, geometric character stand-ins or unapproved artwork may be presented as final production content.
- Missing required art fails closed; no unrelated image or fake sprite fallback is permitted.

## Character animation delivery
Required clips include idle, walk, jog, sprint, jump phases, soft landing, slide phases, directional dodges, interaction, sitting, studying, resting and talking. Each character needs approved clip metadata, stable pivots, consistent ground contact, costume continuity and an in-game preview.

## Environment delivery
Residence, township, campus and city each require distinct background, midground and foreground art. Layers must be authored to the 2.5D camera and parallax plan, with safe composition across the traversable world. Interactive props and hotspots must align to the illustration. Lighting variants must preserve architecture and object identity.

## Approval and integration
The visual production module provides a delivery plan and strict validators. A delivery is not accepted merely because a file exists: it must be local, have exact dimensions and measured bytes, carry creator/licence/source records, receive explicit visual approval and pass in-game review. The current plan intentionally reports all character and district deliverables as not started; no final artwork has yet been supplied.
