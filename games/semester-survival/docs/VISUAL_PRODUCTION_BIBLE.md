# Visual Production Bible

## Locked presentation direction
The cross-game quality contract is defined in [PREMIUM_PRESENTATION_STANDARD.md](PREMIUM_PRESENTATION_STANDARD.md). It governs residence, township, campus, city, character art, animation, UI, weather, effects, audio and device delivery—not just environment backgrounds.
The game retains its 2.5D exploration camera. Its visual identity combines cinematic anime environmental composition with premium anime-game character readability and production discipline. High-resolution source art is authored for crisp presentation; runtime output scales to the device and must not assume every phone can render at native 4K.

## User-approved visual reference direction
The two campus images supplied by the user are the current environment-quality references:
- **Reference A — landscaped institutional courtyard:** monumental but believable academic architecture, a clear central approach, layered flowering trees and planted beds, benches and human-scale details, warm natural daylight, soft atmospheric distance and cinematic depth.
- **Reference B — contemporary campus walkway:** modern glass-and-metal academic buildings, a strong vanishing point, ordered tree planting, integrated seating and noticeboards, bright blue-sky daylight, crisp architectural edges, clean material response and polished anime-background finish.

These are quality and composition references, not game assets and not permission to reproduce their exact buildings, signage or location. Translate the finish into a recognisably Kenyan university and Nairobi student world. Do not copy Japanese signage, cherry blossoms, foreign campus identity or ungrounded architecture. Every district needs its own locally credible design language.

## Environment quality bar
Every district must meet the same premium finish while retaining its own credible Kenyan identity. Residence is intimate and lived-in; township is dense but navigable; campus is aspirational and landscaped; city communicates broader urban scale.
- Wide establishing composition with a readable focal destination and intentional foreground, midground and background separation.
- Convincing perspective, architectural scale, coherent windows/doors/stairs, believable pavement and material transitions.
- Rich but controlled vegetation, planted edges, benches, lamps, noticeboards and human-scale props where appropriate.
- Natural light direction, soft contact shadows, atmospheric depth and consistent colour grading.
- High detail must support navigation and storytelling; avoid visual noise, clutter, repeated copy-paste props and over-sharpened synthetic textures.
- Human figures must be authored as consistent character assets, not baked-in anonymous AI crowds. NPCs and the player remain separate from environment plates.
- Every weather/time variant preserves the same architecture, prop positions and camera geometry.

## Production principles
- Character and environment artwork are separate, versioned deliverables.
- Main cast: Jay, Amina, Einstein, Ernest and Sumeya. Existing supporting cast remains.
- Amina and Sumeya require distinct, beautiful Muslim-appropriate designs with modest contemporary clothing and individually designed hijab styling.
- Jay's locked first-year baseline remains in CHARACTER_BIBLE.md. Four-year maturity remains a separate deferred milestone.
- Characters require model sheets, expression sheets, animation atlases, clip coverage, in-game scale review and approval before integration.
- Each district requires layout paintover, background/midground/foreground art, prop artwork, lighting variants, hotspot review and credits.
- No generated placeholder art, geometric character stand-ins or unapproved artwork may be presented as final production content. User-directed Gemini artwork is allowed as a production candidate only after the user approves the design and confirms the applicable usage rights; it must pass consistency, transparency, atlas and in-game animation checks. Do not misrepresent generated assets as hand-drawn or commissioned work.
- Missing required art fails closed; no unrelated image or fake sprite fallback is permitted.

## Character animation delivery
Required clips include idle, walk, jog, sprint, jump phases, soft landing, slide phases, directional dodges, interaction, sitting, studying, resting and talking. Each character needs approved clip metadata, stable pivots, consistent ground contact, costume continuity and an in-game preview.

## Environment delivery
Residence, township, campus and city each require distinct background, midground and foreground art. Layers must be authored to the 2.5D camera and parallax plan, with safe composition across the traversable world. Interactive props and hotspots must align to the illustration. Lighting variants must preserve architecture and object identity.

## Approval and integration
The visual production module provides a delivery plan and strict validators. A delivery is not accepted merely because a file exists: it must be local, have exact dimensions and measured bytes, carry creator/licence/source records, receive explicit visual approval and pass in-game review. The current plan intentionally reports all character and district deliverables as not started; no final artwork has yet been supplied.
