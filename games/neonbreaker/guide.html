🧱 Neon Breaker — Walkthrough

Objective & Rules

Smash all 5×10 glowing neon bricks without letting the ball drop past your paddle. Top 2 rows require 2 hits; bottom 3 rows break in 1 hit (scoring 50/40/30/20/10 pts per row).

  • Lives & Escalation: Start with 3 lives (max 5). Clearing a board advances the level and increases ball launch speed (3 + level × 0.3).

Controls

  • Keyboard: ←/→ or A/D move paddle; Space launch ball / start / restart; P/Esc pause; M mute; F fullscreen.
  • Mouse & Touch: Move mouse or drag finger horizontally to track the paddle 1:1; tap to launch.

Power-Ups & Strategy

  • 5 Drops (20% chance): 3B Multi-ball, W Wide Paddle (+30px up to 180px), L Laser (+100 bonus pts), + Extra Life, and S Slow (0.7× ball speed).
  • Combos & Saves: Consecutive brick hits build up to a 5× multiplier. High score saves to localStorage (neonBreakerHighScore).
