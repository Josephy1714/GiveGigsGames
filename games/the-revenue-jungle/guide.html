🌴 The Revenue Jungle — Walkthrough

Objective & Zones

Sprint with twin explorer cats across 4 progressive zones—Emerald Canopy (0+), Golden Ruins (450+), Twilight Shrine (1,100+), and Neon Apex (2,000+).

  • Obstacles: Jump (or double-jump) over Vines and Quicksand; slide under (or double-jump over) hovering Fog Drones.
  • Coins & Shields: Chain Gold Coins within 2.6s for up to a 5× multiplier, and stack up to 2 Emerald Shields (each absorbs 1 crash).

Controls & Saves

  • Keyboard: Space/W/↑ Jump & Double-Jump; S/↓ Slide (or Fast-Fall mid-air into slide); P/Esc pause; F fullscreen.
  • Touch: Tap canvas or mobile Jump/Slide buttons.
  • Saves: Best score saves in localStorage (the-revenue-jungle:best).
