⚔️ GraveGain 1D: The Ley-Line March — Walkthrough

Objective & Modes

Walk strictly east along a 1D ley-line (500 tiles across 5 sectors: Crash Flats, Whisper Groves, Sparkite Cut, Ash Gate, Relay Approach), defeat each sector boss, and unlock endless Echo Drift.

  • Modes: Turn-Based or Real-Time solo play, plus optional Ley-Line Race (?match=) and Horde (?mmorpg=) multiplayer.

Controls & Saves

  • Keyboard: D/→ step east; Space/J attack; K/1 ability; H/2 potion; W/0 wait 1 tick; Q speed (1×/2×/0.5×); G Giggles Help; P/Esc pause.
  • Touch: One-thumb Crypt Line (tap/hold left to step/march, tap/hold right to attack/ritual) + Ritual Tap Bar.
  • Saves: Stored in localStorage under gravegain1d_save_v1.
