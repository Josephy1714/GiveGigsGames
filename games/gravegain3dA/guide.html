🏰 GraveGain3DA — Walkthrough

Objective & Modes

Explore procedural first-person 3D dungeons across a 10-Mission Campaign or Endless Descent, draft RoguelikePerks, and upgrade your LuckyStarShip hub.

  • Modes: Realtime, Chrono-Lock, and Turn-Based combat tempos, plus AI Spectator Mode (V) and optional netplay.

Controls

  • Keyboard & Mouse: WASD/Arrows move; Mouse look (Pointer Lock optional); Left-Click 3-hit melee or ranged attack; Right-Click block / off-hand skill; Space jump (Human jetpack, Dwarf double-jump, Elf glide; Turn-Based wait); F race ability; H quick-eat; Q potion; Z cycle weapons; 1–3 weapon slots; E pick up drop; I inventory; V toggle AI spectator; P/Esc pause.
  • Touch: Dual-zone touch joystick, look drag, and 6 action buttons.

Tips & Saves

  • Combat Tips: Smash props for extra gold and loot, drink potions (Q) around 45% HP rather than waiting for 1-hit range, and defeat the floor boss to unlock the safespace descent portal.
  • Saves: Stored in browser localStorage alongside shared GraveGain story progress.
