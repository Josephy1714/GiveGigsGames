🎯 GraveGain 3D C: Arena FPS — Walkthrough

3 Modes & 4 Arenas

Built with Babylon.js across 4 arenas: Neon Courtyard, Void Spires, Magma Foundry, and Cargo Shipment.

Player ControlFirst-person arena FPS with bunny-hopping, jump pads, 4 weapons, and 2 upgradable squad companions.
Auto-Battler RogueliteSpectate an AI hero bot across 3 lives; spend earned scrap on 10 persistent upgrades saved in gravegain3dc_roguelite_v1.
RTS SquadTop-down tactical camera: click the floor to set squad rally points and spawn friendly bots.

Controls & Arsenal

  • Keyboard & Mouse: WASD move/strafe (no Arrow keys); Mouse look (Pointer Lock); Space jump/bunny-hop; Shift dash; Left-Click fire; 1–4 or Wheel switch weapons (Devastator, Vortex, Shotgun, Electro); Q toggle companions Follow/Hold; E upgrade aimed companion (rank 0–3 using scrap); Esc release pointer.
  • Touch (Player Mode): Virtual move pad, drag-look, and Jump/Fire/Dash buttons.
