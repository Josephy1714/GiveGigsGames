🎯 GraveGain 3D C: LuckyStarShip420 Campaign

Colony Campaign · 5 Simulator Exercises

You are a human soldier aboard the LuckyStarShip420, the colony ship crossing into the GraveGain universe. The destination planet is unknown, so the crew trains for varied terrain, hostile soldiers, and possible alien animals. Wildlife is shown as glowing wireframe threat models because this is a combat simulator.

All five exercises are unlocked from the title screen. Choose Begin Colony Campaign to start at the drop zone, or select any exercise card to deploy there immediately. Each exercise has staged enemy pushes with a fresh briefing between contacts. Completing a selected exercise continues through the later drills. Your two squad companions fight beside you.

Five Exercises · All Unlocked

Each briefing is a staged combat exercise. Clear a push to trigger the next contact; the map adds mission-only cover and route features for that deployment. Starting any exercise continues the campaign through the later exercises.

01 · Landing Zone KiloNeon Courtyard. Probe: two riflemen approach from the central deck. Reinforcement: two more cross the north lane. Emergency-pod shells create cover on the south approach; beacon pylons mark the landing corridor.
02 · Cargo BreachCargo Shipment. Three soldiers establish ground-level crossfire through open freight lanes. After they fall, two cyan wireframe fauna appear on upper container roofs. Jump pads and the cargo ramps give access to the high route.
03 · Unknown EcologyVoid Spires. Two fauna contacts approach from the south deck, followed by two entering from the west and east high spires. Added stepping platforms form an optional center route above the void.
04 · Foundry RaidMagma Foundry. Two soldiers and one fauna screen the relay. A second wave splits two soldiers to the west catwalk and two fauna to the east flank. Use the service catwalks to cross the lava trenches.
05 · Colony ShieldNeon Courtyard. Three soldiers set crossfire as two fauna rush the barricade gaps. The final wave sends two soldiers and two fauna from opposite sides toward the landing corridor.
Other ModesPlayer Control offers a free arena match, Auto-Battler is a 3-life spectator roguelite, and RTS Squad uses a top-down rally camera.

The campaign draws from Neon Courtyard, Void Spires, Magma Foundry, and Cargo Shipment.

Controls & Arsenal

  • Keyboard & Mouse: WASD move/strafe (no Arrow keys); Mouse look (Pointer Lock); Space jump/bunny-hop, then press Jump again while pressing into a wall in midair to spring off; Shift dash; Left-Click fire; 1–4 or Wheel switch weapons (Devastator, Vortex, Shotgun, Electro); Q toggle companions Follow/Hold; E upgrade aimed companion (rank 0–3 using scrap); Esc release pointer, F toggle fullscreen, P open Player Control stats.
  • Touch (Campaign and Player Control): Virtual move pad, drag-look, and Jump/Fire/Dash buttons.
