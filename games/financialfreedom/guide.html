Advance month by month until your Net Worth reaches your Financial Independence target (FI Number), while keeping Cash above $0 to avoid Chapter 7 Bankruptcy.

Controls

  • Mouse & Touch: Switch across 7 tabs (Dashboard, Budget Allocator, Career & Gigs, Investments, Debt & Housing, Taxes & Insurance, Terms & Achievements), adjust sliders, and click Next Month (or swipe left on mobile's Life Deck / tap the quick-action bar).
  • Keyboard: Press F to toggle fullscreen.

Core Systems & Strategy

  • Employer Match First: Contribute at least 4% to your 401(k) to capture the full employer match before putting extra cash into Roth IRA, S&P 500, or Crypto.
  • Debt & Housing: Use the Avalanche strategy to eliminate high-interest credit card debt early. You can buy a $250k home once you have $15k for the down payment.
  • Career & Hustles: Enroll in the $1,200 6-month training program (+25% salary) or toggle the Rideshare side hustle (+$400/mo, -20 energy).
  • Macro Cycles & Life Events: Markets rotate annually (Normal, Bull, Bear, High Inflation) with a 15% monthly chance of random events and 2026 IRS tax brackets. Unlock all 6 achievements on your way to FI.
  • Saves: Session-only simulation (no localStorage persistence).
