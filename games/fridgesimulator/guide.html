🥶 Fridge Simulator — Walkthrough

Objective & Controls

Survive 30 days as a global food coordinator starting with $1,000. Keep unlocking household countries fed by buying from 5 suppliers, stocking 12-slot fridges, and balancing Protein (Pro), Carbohydrates (Carb), and Vitamins (Vit).

  • Mouse & Touch: Browse supplier tabs, buy food into your central pantry/inventory, click an item then click an empty + fridge slot (or use Auto-Stock), and switch mobile tabs (Shop, Fridges, Ledger, Plan).
  • Keyboard: Space or N advance to Next Day; P or Esc pause/resume; M mute; F fullscreen.

5 Food Suppliers & Catalog

  • Grocery Store (1.0×): Full reliable catalog (~133 foods across Produce, Dairy & Fats, Bakery, Meals & Staples, Seafood, Desserts, and Drinks).
  • Wholesale (0.7×): Bulk discounts on high-volume staples—great once India and Mexico unlock.
  • Food Pantry (0.5×): Half-price staples when budgets tighten.
  • Charity & Dumpster: Intended as low/zero-cost emergency sources (in the current build, both apply a 0.5× multiplier in economy.js, so compare prices against Wholesale and Pantry).

Nutrition, Streaks & Survival Math

  • Daily Drain: Each day, families lose -15 from each of the 3 nutrient bars (Pro, Carb, Vit). When a fridge has matching preferred food, eating it restores hunger and builds your feedStreak (2+ preferred feeds award +$2 × preferredFeeds bonus).
  • Starvation Penalty: If no food is stocked, family members lose 20 + 5 × depletedNutrients hunger and die (💀) at 0. Surviving members generate daily tax income proportional to alive headcount.
  • Win & Scoring: Reach Day 30 with at least 1 survivor. Score = (day − 1)×100 + alive×10 − deaths×25 + feedStreak×15 + maxCombo×10.
💡 Tip: Check the Tomorrow's Plan panel before advancing each day, and prioritize high-Protein + high-Carb staples alongside Vitamin-rich produce so none of the 3 nutrient bars hit zero.
