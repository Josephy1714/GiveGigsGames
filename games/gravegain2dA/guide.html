⚔️ GraveGain2DA — Walkthrough

Objective & Modes

Battle through procedural 2.5D-lit dark fantasy dungeons across a 10-Mission Campaign, Endless Dungeon, and 3 Daily Mission Logs, then spend Gold at the LuckyStarShip hub.

  • 3 Temporal Modes: Realtime (0.5×–2×), Chrono-Lock (time advances as you act), and Turn-Based.
  • Difficulties: Easy, Normal, or Nightmare, plus optional netplay via URL params.

Controls

  • Keyboard: WASD move (Arrows also step in Turn-Based); Arrow Keys/Space dodge roll (wait in Turn-Based); J race jump/jetpack; Shift sprint; Left-Click primary attack; Right-Click timed block (0.22s parry window); F race ability; E interact; G grenade; K shove; 1/2/3 switch tempo; P/Esc pause.
  • Touch: Floating left crypt-stick with Tap:ON and Target:ON toggles, action runes, and bag/craft drawer.

Races, Classes & LuckyStarShip

  • 4 Races × 4 Classes: Choose Human, Elf, Dwarf, or Orc paired with Warrior, Tank, Support, or Mage.
  • LuckyStarShip Hub: Upgrade Quarters (Lv 1–6), grow 3 Botany crops, play the Repair microcircuit puzzle, convert Gold ↔ $UUSD, and listen to Giggles' Lore Codex.
  • Saves: Stored in localStorage under GraveGain2D_Save and gravegain_shared_story_progress_v1.
