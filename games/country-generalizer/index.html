    🧭 How to Play CountryGeneralizer

    Identify the mystery country from the Top 100 most populous nations on Earth using generalized national profiles across 36 dimensions.

    • Start Small: Round 1 reveals only a couple of clues (e.g. a Tradle-style Export Portfolio or staple diet/biome).
    • 5 Guesses: Each wrong guess reduces your potential score by 1,000 pts (5,000 → 4,000 → 3,000 → 2,000 → 1,000) and unlocks another dimension factoid.
    • Geo-Radar + Trait Telemetry: Every wrong guess plots on the world map with distance, compass bearing, region match, population rank arrow (↑/↓), income tier, and climate hint.
    • Hourly, Daily & Weekly Resets: Compete on synchronized UTC resets! Weekly mode is a 3-Country Marathon with cumulative scoring.
    • Sets & Decks: Switch between Top 100, Top 30 Giants, Challenger 31–100, Regional sets, or specialized Dimension Decks (Tradle Economy, Bio-Atlas, Food & Culture, Mystery Blind).

    Keyboard shortcuts: Press / to focus search, ↑/↓ to pick a country, Enter to submit, Esc to close panels.
