🌍 CountryGeneralizer — Walkthrough

Core Rules (5 Guesses, Progressive Intel)

  • Target Pool: Every challenge selects a mystery country from the Top 100 most populous countries on Earth (from India #1 down to Switzerland #100), or a filtered regional/tier Set of your choice.
  • Little Info Each Round: You start with only 1–2 teaser factoids. Every wrong guess reduces your potential score by 1,000 pts (5,000 → 4,000 → 3,000 → 2,000 → 1,000) and unlocks 1 more dimension factoid.
  • Bonus Intel Peek: Stuck? Spend 250 potential points (up to 3 times per round) to reveal an extra clue without burning a guess.
  • Geo-Radar + Trait Telemetry: Wrong guesses plot on the vector world map with distance, compass bearing, region match, population rank arrow (↑/↓), income tier, and climate zone.

The 36 Generalization Dimensions

  • 🍲 Food & Diet (6): Staple diet, cuisine & consumption leaders, meat/seafood/dairy intake, beverages, fats/spices, caloric profile.
  • 🦁 Animal Life (6): Livestock ratios, wild mammals, avian & marine life, endemic fauna, fisheries/working animals, biodiversity rank.
  • 🌿 Plants & Biomes (6): Dominant biomes, top crops by tonnage, forest canopy & timber, native cash flora, arable land & irrigation, climate & seasons.
  • 📦 Trade & Economy (6): Visual Tradle-style Export Portfolio bar, import dependencies, electricity mix, GDP per capita tier, labor split, minerals & natural wealth.
  • 👥 Demographics (6): Population scale & density, median age, urbanization, linguistic families, religious makeup, literacy & life expectancy.
  • 🧭 Society & Geo (6): Roads/rail & driving side, currency/timezones/units, vernacular housing, sports & pastimes, internet/tech footprint, borders & coastline.

Resets, Sets, Decks & Leaderboards

  • Hourly Sprint: New country & clue deck every UTC hour.
  • Daily Challenge: Synchronized daily puzzle resetting at 00:00 UTC.
  • Weekly 3x Marathon: A 3-country cumulative gauntlet resetting every Monday 00:00 UTC.
  • Sets & Decks: Play Top 100, Top 30 Giants, Challenger 31–100, or Regional Sets across 5 Dimension Decks (including Tradle Trade, Bio-Atlas, and Mystery Blind +25%), plus Purist Mode (+20%) with map hints disabled.
  • Passport & Friend Tokens: Every country you face unlocks in your 100-Country Passport for full 36-dimension study, and Share Result includes a CG1|... token friends can paste into their Leaderboard.
