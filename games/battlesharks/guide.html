🦈 BattleSharks — Walkthrough

Objective & Modes

Swim through a 3D Three.js abyssal arena across 5 depth biomes (0–320m), devour prey to heal and grow, evolve with cybernetic mutations, and defeat 6 escalating mecha-bosses.

  • Arena Roles: Play as Shark or Human (Sub Gunner).
  • Hub Modes: My Aquarium (6 shark species care & tank builder), Base Builder (10×6 grid), Atlantis Colosseum (8 tournament modes), and Human Arsenal (7 weapons, 8 diving gear).

Controls

  • Shark Mode: W/S or ↑/↓ swim/brake; A/D or ←/→ turn; Q/E or right-drag dive/climb; Space Jet Dash; Left-Click/F fire lasers; B/Right-Click Bite Lunge; Tab/U Upgrade Lab; I Inventory; L Flashlight; C Sonar; P Photo Mode; Esc pause.
  • Human Sub Mode: Adds V first-person toggle, R vent turret heat, T thermal visor, 1/2 Harpoon Bolts / Sonic Disruptor.
  • Touch: Virtual swim joystick + Dive/Climb buttons + Laser & Dash triggers.

Biomes, Bosses & Mutations

  • 5 Biomes: BS-07 Shallows (0–45m), Baroque Coral Reef (45–95m), Sunken Ruins (95–145m), Deep Trenches (145–220m), and Abyssal Caverns (220–320m).
  • 6 Bosses (starting at 600 score): Leviathan Mecha-Sub, Kraken, Megalodon, Mosasaur, Colossus, and Scourge.
  • 10 Lab Upgrades + 9 Bionics: Unlock Twin Lasers, Jet Thrusters, Plasma Shield, Electric Aura, Acidic Blood, Armored Scales, Homing Bio-Missiles, and aquarium turrets/regen.

Saves

Progress persists in localStorage under battlesharks_v2_state, battlesharks_coins, battlesharks_shop, and battlesharks_highscore keys.
