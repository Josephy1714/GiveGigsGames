🦈 BattleSharks 2D — Walkthrough

Objective & Loop

Survive the BS-07 Aquarium Testing Complex: devour 7 prey species to heal and gain XP, harvest Cyber-Debris (⚙️) and Mutagens (🧪) for 10 R&D Lab upgrades, and defeat the Robo-Kraken boss at 3,500+ score.

  • Growth Tiers: Evolve from Pup → Juvenile → Hunter → Predator → Apex Megalodon (+0.18 scale, +20 max HP, full heal per level).

Controls

  • Keyboard & Mouse: WASD/Arrows or mouse movement steers your shark; Left-Click fires Laser Cannon (once unlocked); Space Jet Dash (75-frame cooldown); E opens/closes the R&D Lab; P/Esc pause; M mute; F fullscreen.
  • Touch: Drag anywhere to swim and tap to trigger a 20-frame bite lunge and laser shot.

R&D Lab & Boss Strategy

  • Early Buys: Unlock Laser Cannon (20 ⚙️) and Jet Thrusters (30 ⚙️ + 10 🧬) first so you can snipe Cyber Divers and Mecha Sentinels instead of tanking mines.
  • Robo-Kraken (1,000 HP): Spawns at 3,500 score with 5-way spreads and sweep beams. Ramming deals 60 dmg to the boss (8 to you), and Plasma Shield + Homing Missiles shred its HP bar for a +10,000-point win bonus before endless play continues.
  • Saves: High score saves to localStorage (battlesharks2_highscore).
