💎 Treasure Hunters — Walkthrough

Objective & Quotas

Aim and launch a swinging 3D claw across timed 35+ second shifts to meet each stage's dollar quota (Stage 1 starts at $2,500).

  • Targets: Gold ($800–$1,249), Diamonds ($2,000, fast reel), moving Leads ($1,500), Mystery Crates (+$1,200×mult, +6s time, or 8s 1.85× Turbo Winch), heavy Rocks ($140, slow reel), and Mines (explode nearby rocks within 2.3 units, -4s penalty).
  • Between-Round Upgrades: Choose from Winch Turbo (1.35× speed), Value Scanner (+0.25× payout), and Shift Extension (+8s per round).

Controls & Saves

  • Controls: Press Space, S, ↓, or click/tap the canvas to drop the claw; P/Esc pause; F fullscreen.
  • Saves: Best haul saves in localStorage (treasure-hunters:best).
