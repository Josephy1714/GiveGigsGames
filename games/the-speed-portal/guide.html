🌀 The Speed Portal — Walkthrough

Objective & Zones

Bank 360° around the interior of a 3D neon warp tunnel through 4 endless distance zones: Warmup (0m+), Overclock (250m+), Hyperdrive (650m+), and Singularity (1,200m+).

  • Hazards: Dodge single and twin Red Arc Barriers plus Rotating Laser Spokes (from 250m onward).
  • Speed Rings & Shields: Threading Emerald Speed Rings grants +35m and a 1.6s boost; every 3 rings recharges your Energy Shield (starts ON). Grazing barriers (<0.82 rad) awards +25m.

Controls & Saves

  • Steer with A/D, ←/→, or the touch arrows. Gravity pulls your ship back toward the wall you steer toward.
  • Tap Space or the touch Jump button to hop over low barriers. Hold to dive almost to the center. Cyan ramps launch you into the tunnel, where you can encounter gold air mines.
  • P/Esc pauses and F toggles fullscreen.
  • Saves: Best distance saves in localStorage (the-speed-portal:best).
