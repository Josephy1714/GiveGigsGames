🕶️ AssassinAnimals — Walkthrough

Objective & Modes

Infiltrate procedurally generated high-security facilities, eliminate VIP Scientist targets or steal/hack the Exit Keycard, and escape via the Elevator.

  • Modes: Endless Free Run infiltration plus 13 authored Contracts (such as First Blood, Ghost Protocol, 90-Second Sprint, and Phantom Floor) graded from Bronze to Phantom.

Controls

  • Keyboard: WASD/Arrows move; Left-Click/Space strike; Right-Click/Q toss coin; E silent takedown / steal uniform / hold 2s hack terminal or 1.5s pickpocket; G drag body; Shift/F signature ability; 1–5 gadgets (Medkit, Tranq Dart, Smoke Bomb, Noise Decoy, Adrenal Surge); P/Esc pause.
  • Touch: Left joystick prowl, right look/tap VIP swipe zone, plus contextual FANG/STEAL/DRAG/COIN and action buttons.

9 Operatives & Bio-Lab Mutations

  • 9 Agents: Cyber-Panther, Mech-Gorilla, Nano-Cobra, Shadow-Hawk, Psycho-Badger, Cyber-Octopus, Jet-Kangaroo, Electro-Beaver, and Heavy-Rhino—each with distinct speed, stealth, noise, primary attack, and signature ability.
  • Stealth Rules: Stay out of guard vision cones and laser tripwires, hide inside foliage, drag neutralized bodies (G) into lockers/dumpsters, and watch out for white-dot Enforcers who see through stolen uniforms.
  • Elevator Bio-Lab: Collect DNA splices (🧬) on each floor to buy 6 mutations: Chitin Plating, Adrenaline Injector, Carbon Claws, Thermal Retinas, Silencer Pads, and Synaptic Coolers.
