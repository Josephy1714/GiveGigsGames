🏛️ Temple of Lost Revenue — Walkthrough

Objective & Loop

Navigate procedural multi-floor 3D mazes (scaling up to 15×15) as the Blue Cat. Collect all 5 gold Leads (+$500 each) on a floor to unlock the emerald Exit Portal and descend deeper.

  • Clock & Hearts: You start with 3 hearts and 60 seconds. Each cleared floor awards +$500 × floor and +14 seconds (capped at 80s).

Controls & Ability

  • Keyboard: WASD or Arrow Keys step through maze corridors; Space/Shift/E trigger Decipher Pulse (5s cooldown, stuns nearby Red Audit Golems for 2.6s); P/Esc pause; F fullscreen.
  • Touch: Virtual thumb-stick moves; ACT triggers Decipher Pulse.
  • Saves: Best score persists in localStorage (temple-of-lost-revenue:best).
