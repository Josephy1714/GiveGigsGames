🎨 Sound Painter — Walkthrough

Overview

Paint colorful music onto an interactive 8×6 canvas grid. Every column maps to a C4–C5 diatonic note, and each of the 6 rows pairs a distinct color with a synthesizer voice.

  • Tabs: Switch between 🎨 Paint and 🎛 Instruments, or use the bottom thumb palette to pick Free or lock a specific instrument row.

Controls

  • Mouse & Touch: Click or drag across tiles to paint/erase notes; multi-touch adds an octave-higher shimmer! Hold Play Pattern to loop every 800ms, or tap Undo / Redo.
  • Keyboard: Space Play All once (100ms per column); R reset grid; Ctrl/Cmd+Z Undo (+Shift Redo); P/Esc pause. When canvas is focused, use Arrow Keys + Enter/Space to paint.

Tips & Saves

  • Tip: Build a rhythmic anchor on the bottom rows first, then drag diagonal melodies across the upper rows.
  • Saves: Active tile count record is stored in localStorage (soundpainter_tilesCount).
