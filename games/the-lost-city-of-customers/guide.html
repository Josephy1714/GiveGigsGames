🗺️ The Lost City of Customers — Walkthrough

Objective & Scanner Cues

Sweep an 8×8 3D valley grid using limited battery scans to uncover buried client databases (4+ per sector, scaling up to 8).

  • Hot/Cold Feedback: Exact Hit (d = 0) awards +250 + streak×50 pts and +3 scans; HOT (d ≤ 1.5) turns orange and reveals nearby target crystals; WARM (d ≤ 3.0) turns teal; COLD turns slate.
  • Sector 2+ Static Decoys: Purple decoy tiles drain 1 extra scan and reset your streak—use triangulation from earlier pings to avoid blind guesses.

Controls & Saves

  • Mouse & Touch: Click or tap any 8×8 grid tile to scan.
  • Keyboard: WASD/Arrows move the diamond cursor; Space/Enter scan; P/Esc pause; F fullscreen.
  • Saves: High score saves to localStorage (the-lost-city-of-customers:best).
