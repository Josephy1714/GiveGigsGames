💀 Demo Lichdom — Walkthrough

Objective

Command an undead skeleton army as a modern demolition necromancer across 3 escalating phases: destroy the Old Church (300 HP), the Factory (500 HP), and the Skyscraper (1,000 HP) without letting your HP reach 0.

Controls

  • Keyboard: W / A / D or Arrow Keys move (note: S is bound to Summon Skeleton, so use ArrowDown or Left-Click steering to move south); S summon skeleton (-25 Mana); Space Blink teleport 100px toward cursor (unlocked by Spectral Dash); 1 / 2 / 3 pick level-up upgrade; P / Esc pause; F fullscreen.
  • Mouse: Left-Click casts a Skull Spell (-10 Mana, 15 base dmg) toward the cursor, and holding Left-Click steers your necromancer toward the cursor.
  • Touch: Left-side virtual stick moves; right-half tap casts toward the tap; bottom Decree Bar buttons trigger Cast, Summon, Blink, and Pause.

The 3 Demolition Phases

⛪ Phase 1: Old Church (300 HP)Passive brick structure with no active defenses. Great for building early XP and summoning your initial 3 skeletons.
🏭 Phase 2: Factory (500 HP)Periodically drops falling bricks (🧱, 10 dmg) every 120 frames. Keep moving while skeletons chip away at the walls.
🏙️ Phase 3: Skyscraper (1,000 HP)Drops bricks twice as fast (every 60 frames) and spawns 3 City Defenders (👮, 50 HP, 8–10 dmg) to hunt you.

Level-Up Upgrades (5 Total)

  • Mana Mastery: +30 max mana and +50% faster mana regeneration.
  • Lich Lord: +2 maximum active skeletons (base cap 3) and +5 skeleton HP.
  • Void Bolt: +15 skull spell damage (30 total) and adds piercing shots.
  • Spectral Dash: +30% movement speed and unlocks Space Blink teleport (180-frame cooldown).
  • Death Ward: +25 max HP and +0.1 HP/frame passive regeneration.

Strategy, Scoring & Saves

  • Scoring: +15 pts per spell hit on buildings, +10 pts per skeleton strike, +25 pts per defender hit (+50 kill, or combo×15 at 3+ combo), and +1,000×phase when each building collapses.
  • Tips: Keep your skeleton cap filled whenever Mana ≥ 25—skeletons tank defenders and deal steady siege damage while you dodge bricks. Pick Void Bolt or Lich Lord early to melt Phase 2 and Phase 3.
  • Saves: High score is stored in browser localStorage under demolichdom_hs.
