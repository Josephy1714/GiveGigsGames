🚀 Starship Simulator — Walkthrough

Objective & Modes

Command a Three.js 3D starship across 6 planets (Ember, Azure, Viridian, Cryo, Obsidian, Aurelia + moon Aurelia-IV), 3 orbital stations, 85 asteroids, and 4 salvage wrecks.

  • Modes: Freeform Space Sandbox, 10-lesson Flight Academy (T), 3D Cockpit (C) + 4 camera views, Planetary Surface Rover (L), and optional Ironman permadeath.

Controls

  • Flight Keyboard: W/S or ↑/↓ pitch; A/D or ←/→ yaw; Q/E roll; +/- throttle; Shift afterburner; X/Ctrl air-brake; Space/Click fire; F cycle weapon; M missile; G flares; Tab target lock; C cockpit; N Star Map; L Land/Dock; P Autopilot; H Auto-Fire; V Mouse-Steer; 1–4 fleet formations; F1–F4 squadron orders; T Academy.
  • Surface Rover: WASD/Arrows drive; Space Jump Jets; E interact with anomalies/probes; L launch back to orbit.
  • Touch & Gamepad: Mobile fly-stick + right-half turret drag + FIRE/BOOST/MSL/Land buttons; native gamepad axes 0–2 supported.

Ships, Weapons & Progression

  • 6 Ship Classes: Vanguard (free), Dart (8.5k CR), Gunship (45k), Corvette (180k), Frigate (950k), and Sovereign (15M CR, +120 CR/s passive income), plus 6 Upgrade Tracks (Lv 0–5) and 4 Weapons (Plasma, Rapid, Ion EMP, Mining).
  • Combat & Contracts: Accept up to 3 Station Contracts at once, mine glowing ore asteroids, and prepare for the Void Dreadnought boss every 14 kills.
  • Note: Uses ES modules—launch over HTTP (npm run tester:bridge, npm run serve, or hosted web) rather than file://.
