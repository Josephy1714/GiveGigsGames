🎮 Template Demo — Walkthrough

Overview

A playable demonstration of the standard 4weird HTML5 canvas game shell, showing movement bounds, color cycling, pause/restart overlays, and localStorage high-score persistence.

  • Scoring: Earn +1 point every 60 frames alive and +10 points each time you cycle the orb through its 5 neon colors.

Controls & Saves

  • Keyboard & Touch: Arrow Keys or WASD (or touch drag) move the orb; Space (or tap) cycles colors; P/Esc pause.
  • Saves: Best score saves to localStorage under template-demo_highScore.
