💀 GraveGain 3D — Walkthrough

Objective & Modes

Fight through first-person 3D Three.js dungeons across a 10-Mission Campaign or Endless Dungeon Run, drafting from 15 RoguelikePerks as you level up.

  • Races & Classes: 4 Races (Human Shield Wall + jetpack, Elf Nature Burst, Dwarf Stone Form + double-jump, Orc Rage Burst) × 4 Classes (Warrior sword, Tank warhammer, Mage staff, Support chem-gun).
  • Modes: Realtime, Chrono-Lock, and Turn-Based, plus optional Spire Siege multiplayer (?match=).

Controls & Hub

  • Keyboard & Mouse: WASD/Arrows move; Mouse look (Pointer Lock); Left-Click primary attack; Right-Click hold block (75% DR, Tank 90%); Shift sprint; Space jump/wait; F race ability (7s CD); Q potion (+50% max HP); G Giggles Help; P/Esc pause.
  • Touch: Dual-zone left joystick + right touch-look + action buttons.
  • LuckyStarShip: Manage Quarters, Botany (4 seeds incl. Sparkite), Armory (5 upgrades), Repair, Exchange, and Lore.
