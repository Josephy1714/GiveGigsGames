🕯️ GraveGain 2D — Walkthrough

Objective & Modes

Explore procedural 2D dungeons across a 10-Mission Campaign, Endless Dungeon Run, and 3 Daily Mission Logs, and spend Gold upgrading your LuckyStarShip hub.

  • 3 Temporal Modes: Realtime (0.5×–2×), Chrono-Lock (slow-motion idle, full speed on input), and Turn-Based.

Controls & Hub

  • Keyboard & Mouse: WASD/Arrows move; Space dodge roll (wait in Turn-Based); Shift sprint (or race ability if pressed stationary); F race ability; Left-Click melee sweep; Right-Click timed block (0.22s parry window); 1/2/3 tempo; G Giggles Help; P/Esc pause.
  • Touch: Floating left crypt-stick + tap-to-move/loot + A/B runes + Bag/Grow drawer (use the Bag drawer button to drink potions).
  • LuckyStarShip & Saves: Upgrade Quarters (Lv 1–6), plant Botany crops, repair microcircuits, exchange Gold/$UUSD, and read the Lore Codex. Saves in GraveGain2D_Save.
