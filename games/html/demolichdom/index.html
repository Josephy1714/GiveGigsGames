📖 HOW TO PLAY

🏰Three sites, one contractRuin the Church, Factory, then Skyscraper. The last site is guarded.
🧙‍♂️Keep your lich aliveEnemies attack while you work. Move around the site and avoid contact.
🧱Watch the skylineLater sites rain debris. Keep moving when the warning appears.
🖱️Cast a skull boltClick or tap the field to aim. Costs 10 mana; mana regenerates.
💀Build your crewPress S or tap Army. Each skeleton costs 25 mana and attacks nearby threats.
👻Escape with a blinkChoose the Spectral Dash decree to unlock Space / Blink.
⭐Grow strongerDamage buildings and defeat enemies to earn XP. Pick one decree each level.
⚡Chain defeatsTake down enemies quickly to build a combo and raise your score.
