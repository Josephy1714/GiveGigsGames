🌌 Neon Void Runner — Walkthrough

Objective & Hazards

Pilot a cyber runner down a 3-lane digital void, dodging blocks (■), spikes (▲), and barriers (=) as pace scales with distance.

  • Near-Misses & Pickups: Skim past obstacles (<24px gap) for +10m bonuses. Collect ★ Coins (+50m), 5-second Shields (absorbs 1 collision), and Slow-Mo pickups.

Controls & Saves

  • Keyboard: ←/→ or A/D shift lanes; ↑/↓ or W/S adjust speed; Space start/restart; P/Esc pause; M mute; F fullscreen.
  • Touch: Tap left/right canvas halves, swipe horizontally for lanes or vertically for speed, or use on-screen ◀/▶ buttons.
  • Saves: Best distance saves in localStorage (neonVoidRunnerHighScore).
