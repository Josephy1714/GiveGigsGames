🌀 GraveGain5DA — Walkthrough

Objective & Modes

Fight through 10 multiverse floors across 6 parallel Arroyos—Prime, Echo, Dream, Void, Bloom, Static—to reach the Prime Array before Paradox collapse timers overwhelm your run.

  • Classes: Blade of the Graves (Chain Leap), Paradox Warden (Anchor Reality), and Universe Drifter (Free Hop).
  • Multiplayer: Supports optional 8-player Hop-Race overlay when launched with ?mmorpg=.

Controls

  • Keyboard: Space/Enter strike; F attack; U/V hop universe; R class ability; H heal potion; Q/E step; T wait; G Giggles Help draft.
  • Touch: Bottom action bar with STRIKE, HOP, ABIL, VENT (potion), ATTACK, and WAIT.

Paradox, Collapse & Strategy

  • Combo Chains vs. Paradox: Hopping universes swaps enemies and builds your combo chain (up to 8×), adding 8 + chain×6 Paradox (0–100). At 100 Paradox, a doom timer begins (Void: 12 ticks, Static: 8, others: 6); expiring in Void/Static bounces you back to Prime and resets combo.
  • Tip: Clear both foes per floor, use Paradox Warden or calm Wait turns to vent Paradox before jumping into Void/Static, and save potions (H) for brute/necro waves.
