GraveGain2dD: Moonlit Run — Walkthrough

Controls

  • Touch: Use the bottom-left virtual joystick to sprint (right), brake (left), jump/vault (up), and slide or drop through platforms (down). You can also tap the right half of the screen to jump or swipe down to slide.
  • Keyboard: D / → sprint, A / ← brake, W / ↑ / Space jump or vault, S / ↓ slide or drop, P / Esc pause.
  • Gamepad: Left stick or D-Pad to move, South button (A / Cross) to jump or vault, East button (B / Circle) to slide or drop.

Parkour & Hazard Tech

  • Vaulting: Push up as you reach a wooden stump to vault over it with a speed burst and a Flow Combo boost.
  • Sliding: Hold down under turret fireballs and low stone archways (marked SLIDE ↓).
  • Aerial Stomp: Land on top of a skeleton or slime from above to bounce upward, defeat it, and build your combo multiplier.
  • Branching Routes: Hop up the low ledges and jump from a mint-green launch ledge to reach the rooftops. Follow the gold edges to summit caches. Press down to drop to a lower route, or brake to line up a shorter jump.
  • Wall Jumps: When Lira grips a climbwall, press jump again to spring upward.

Roguelite Progression & Crypt Warden

  • Run Drafts: The first 3-card draft opens at 24 moonshards, and later drafts take progressively more shards. Moon Dial lowers the shard requirements. Contracts award sigils after the run and restore a missing heart when completed. Reaching Master rank in Climb, Reach, or Vault unlocks a capstone perk.
  • Permanent Shop: Spend earned sigils between runs to permanently upgrade starting hearts, wards, speed, and draft pacing.
  • Crypt Warden: Every 1,000m, survive a 20-second Warden pursuit for bonus sigils and a combo boost.
